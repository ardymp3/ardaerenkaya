Musician / Sound Engineer

Arda Eren Kaya

Arda Eren Kaya is a sound engineer and musician based in Istanbul, focusing on music production, sound design, and audio post-production. He is currently studying Music Technology at Bahcesehir University. As a freelancer, he has worked on music for theatre productions, game music and sound design, as well as independent music projects. Currently, he is developing his own tools and software for music technology.
